    Welcome to Cartwise 🛒

    Your personal grocery savings assistant

    Save money every week
    Cartwise watches this week’s sales and alerts you when items from your shopping list go on sale — including BOGOs.
    🏷️

    Browse all deals

    See every sale at your Publix, filtered by category, savings type, or BOGO.

    🎯

    Personalized matches

    Add items to your list and we’ll surface deals that match — powered by smart matching that checks titles, brands, and descriptions.

    📧

    Weekly email alerts

    Get notified every Wednesday when your items go on sale, at the email you sign in with.

    Getting set up takes 2 minutes
    Three things to do to get the most out of Cartwise:
    1

    Set your store — search by city, ZIP, or store number. Your store determines which weekly deals you see.

    2

    Build your list — go to My List and add the items you regularly buy. Partial names work great (e.g. “chicken”, “yogurt”).

    3

    Turn on email alerts — visit Notifications and make sure weekly alerts are on. They go to the email you sign in with. New deals drop every Wednesday.

    You’re all set!
    First up: find your Publix store. Once it’s set, your personalized deals will load automatically.
