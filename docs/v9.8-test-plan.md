# CartWise v9.8 — Test Plan (BOGO store rule)

## What changed

Publix BOGO terms depend on the area:
- **Buy one at half price:** Tennessee, North Carolina, Virginia, and the northern parts of Alabama, Georgia and South Carolina.
- **Buy two to get one free:** Florida and the southern parts of AL, GA and SC.

Sources: Clark.com, "Publix BOGO policy"; Supermarket News, 2026-09-25.

| Store state | Rule set automatically |
|---|---|
| FL | Must buy two |
| TN, NC, VA, KY | One at half price. KY isn't named in the sources; it follows the "most states" rule. |
| AL, GA, SC | Rules differ within the state, so the user is asked. Until they answer, Cartwise assumes "buy two", which is true at every Publix. |

- **New preference `bogo_mode`:** `auto`, `half` or `two`. It's set on **My Store**, under "How do BOGO deals work at your store?".
- **First-run question:** if the store is in AL, GA or SC and you haven't answered, a banner on Deals asks: "Can you buy one BOGO item at half price?"
- **Pricing on cards, the modal and the email** (Buy 1 Get 1, Buy 2 Get 2, and other equal Buy N Get N deals):
  - **Half:** "Half price: $4.50 ea, even if you buy 1" · "Save up to $4.50 each"
  - **Two:** "≈ $4.50 ea when you buy 2" · "Save up to $8.99" (Publix's own figure)
- **Unequal deals** like Buy 2 Get 1 have no half-price rule, so they look the same in both modes.

## 1. Automated

```bash
python -m pytest -q tests        # expect 240 passed, 1 skipped (Node)
```

`tests/test_v98.py` has 21 tests: the state map, the effective rule, pricing in both modes (each, per lb, Buy 2 Get 1, non-BOGO), the email, and preference validation. A JS-vs-Python parity check also runs when Node is installed. The browser checks cover a FL store (no banner, buy-two pricing), a TN store (half-price pricing), and a GA store (banner, then "Yes" switches to half-price pricing and hides the banner).

## 2. Staging checks

1. **Your store:** with your real store selected, open **My Store**. **Pass:** the BOGO card shows "Automatic: …" with the right rule for your state.
2. **Card text:** on Deals, find a Buy 1 Get 1 card. **Pass:** its text matches the rule.
3. **Switching:** pick the other option on My Store. **Pass:** the BOGO cards update immediately, and the setting is still there after a reload.
4. **GA banner:** change store to one in **Georgia**, or use a throwaway account. **Pass:** the banner appears on Deals. Answer it; **Pass:** the banner disappears and the My Store card shows your answer.
5. **Email:** set "I can buy one at half price", then Notifications → 🛒 Resend This Week's Deals. **Pass:** BOGO rows say "even if you buy 1". Switch back if that's not your store's rule.

## 3. Production

Deploy as usual. Existing users start on `auto`. Florida stores keep the old "when you buy 2" wording; TN, NC, VA and KY users see half-price wording; AL, GA and SC users get the one-time question.
