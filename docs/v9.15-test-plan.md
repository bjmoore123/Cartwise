# CartWise v9.15 — Test Plan (Wednesday and Thursday ad cycles)

**Flow:** automated tests → staging deploy → checks → Ben approves → production deploy.

## What changed

Publix runs two weekly ad cycles: Wednesday–Tuesday and Thursday–Wednesday (Publix FAQ: each ad posts
around midnight Eastern the morning it starts). Until now every store was checked and emailed on
Wednesday, so a Thursday-cycle store got an alert for last week's ad on the day it ended.

| Area | Change |
|---|---|
| Schedule | v9.15.1: the rule fires **every morning** at 12:00 UTC (7 AM Eastern in winter). Wednesday + Thursday only (v9.15) missed holiday shifts: Thanksgiving 2026 Publix ran the Wednesday stores' 11/18 ad an extra day and posted every new ad on the evening of 11/25. |
| Who gets emailed | Each store emails only when its new ad started today or yesterday (Eastern), and only once per ad (`adsent#<store>#<ad start>` in the metrics table, kept 60 days). Wednesday stores send Wednesday; Thursday stores send Thursday. |
| Retry | A store skipped on Wednesday (Publix down) is picked up on Thursday's run. |
| Site | Thursday-cycle stores' deals on the site now refresh on Thursday, not a week late. |
| Fallback | If a store's ad start date can't be read, the old rule applies: Wednesday only, once a week. |
| Reports | Both runs add into one `run#<Wednesday>` row, so "last weekly run" covers every store. |
| Notice | A one-time popup for people with a store and alerts on: "Your deal alert day may change". It names their store's day ("…starts its ad on Thursdays, so your alert will now arrive on Thursday mornings") from the weekly ad dates the page already loads. Shown once per account per browser; new accounts don't get it; it stops appearing after 1 Feb 2027. |
| Alert dates | v9.15.1: subject and header show the store's ad dates, worked out from all its deals (`shared/adweek.py`): "(Thu 11/26 – Wed 12/2)" and "Weekly ad Thu 11/26 – Wed 12/2". Before, both came from the first matched deal. A row shows its own "Valid …" only when it differs (ends sooner, coupon dates). Same in the plain-text part. |
| Unchanged | One-user resends (Admin, test script) always send. The alert already shows each deal's "Valid … – …" dates. |

## 3. Checks on staging

**T1 · Schedule.**
```bash
aws events describe-rule --name cartwise-staging-weekly --query ScheduleExpression --output text
```
Pass: `cron(0 12 * * ? *)`.

**T2 · A scheduled run on a non-ad day sends nothing.** On Monday or Tuesday (every store's ad started
4+ days ago):
```bash
aws lambda invoke --function-name cartwise-staging-scraper --cli-binary-format raw-in-base64-out \
  --payload '{"source":"aws.events"}' cw_test_out.json >/dev/null; rm -f cw_test_out.json
sleep 90
export PYTHONUTF8=1 PYTHONIOENCODING=utf-8   # Windows: log lines contain ▶
aws logs tail /aws/lambda/cartwise-staging-scraper --since 5m | grep -E "Alerts:|Worker done"
```
Pass: every store shows `Alerts: ad started <date> (Wednesday|Thursday); alerts go out when the next ad
starts` and `no emails this run`; no alert email arrives. The weekday in each line is that store's cycle.

**T3 · One-user resend still sends.** `bash tools/test_emails.sh` step 6, or Admin → resend to yourself.
Pass: the alert arrives.

**T3b · Alert dates.** In the alert from T3: the subject ends with the ad dates with weekdays, the line under the heading reads "Weekly ad <Day M/D> – <Day M/D>", and only deals with different dates show their own "Valid …".

**T4 · Notice.** Hard-reload staging and sign in with an account that has a store and alerts on.
Pass: the popup appears over Deals, naming your store and its day. Got it closes it; reload and it stays
closed. On your phone it fits without scrolling sideways.
